How to read this radar

A protocol is pre-token when its record carries no token: no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol. That is what the record says, never proof it has no token. The watchlist is the 40 pre-token protocols at or above $50M TVL; the board ranks them all, plus the early finds below the floor. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of assets that already trade are set aside: their value is the underlying asset’s, not a new token’s.

Implied value = TVL × the category’s peer multiple (the median market-cap/TVL of live-token protocols in the same category, or the global median where the category has no live peer) × a growth factor from the 7-day TVL change × a maturity factor from how long the protocol has run with no token × a breadth factor for the chains it runs on. No name is ever special-cased.

Farm plans are suggestions, not advice: a token may never be issued, and an airdrop may never come. Never pay anyone to claim for you, and only ever claim from the protocol’s own site.
